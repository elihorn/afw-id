---
title: A Friendly Wave
---

A Friendly Wave (AFW) is a volunteer-run community listening space and event series on Salt Spring Island. Live weekend event series ("seasons") run a few times a year, DJs play vinyl at Cassette Café every Sunday, and the radio show runs weekly on CHIR FM.

The visual character should feel fun, welcoming and unpretentious, and it should convey the energy collaborative project: something in progress, but executed with the care and energy and attention to detail the collective brings to it.
