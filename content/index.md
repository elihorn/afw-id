# Brand Guideline 2.0

<video src="aFW.mp4" autoplay muted loop playsinline></video>

A Friendly Wave (AFW) is a volunteer-run community listening space and event series on Salt Spring Island. Live weekend event series ("seasons") run a few times a year, DJs play vinyl at Cassette Café every Sunday, and the radio show runs weekly on CHIR FM.

The visual character should feel fun, welcoming and unpretentious. It should convey a sense of something in evolving and in progress, while capturing the care, energy and attention to detail the collective brings to it.
