---
title: A Friendly Wave
---

A Friendly Wave (AFW) is a volunteer-run community listening space and radio initiative on Salt Spring Island, BC, presented with Cassette Café. Live weekends ("seasons") run every three-ish months; the radio show runs weekly on CHIR FM.

The identity is one blue, a small set of flat grounds, one monospace typeface, a blocky hand-cut logotype, and loose single-line drawings of a friendly character. It should feel like a hand-made gig poster from a hi-fi shop: warm, plain-spoken, a little odd.
