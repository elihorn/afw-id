title: A Friendly Wave
---

A Friendly Wave (AFW) is a volunteer-run community listening space and radio initiative on Salt Spring Island, BC, presented with Cassette Café. Live weekends ("seasons") run every three-ish months; the radio show runs weekly on CHIR FM.

The identity is one blue, a small set of flat grounds, one monospace typeface, a blocky hand-cut logotype, and loose single-line drawings of a friendly character. It should feel like a hand-made gig poster from a hi-fi shop: warm, plain-spoken, a little odd.

## Principles

1. **Blue is always present.** Every piece carries blue, as ink, as ground, or as the shadow of a duotone.
2. **Flat colour, no effects.** Solid grounds and bands, square corners, no gradients, shadows or outlines. Texture comes only from the photo screen and the hand-drawn line.
3. **One typeface, two cases.** Everything typeset is Panchi Mono Regular. ALL CAPS for labels, names and headers; sentence case for statements.
4. **The logotype is drawn, not typed.** Never set "a friendly wave" in a font to imitate it. Use the logo files.
5. **Anchor to the edge.** The mark sits flush to an edge of the page, usually bleeding off the bottom-right corner.
6. **Information as a timetable.** Dates, times, places and names are laid out in aligned columns with hairline rules, like a programme.
